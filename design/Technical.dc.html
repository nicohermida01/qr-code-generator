Why this

Owns the "built by devs, for devs" niche. INPUT / OUTPUT framing makes the download formats first-class instead of an afterthought, and it is the most memorable of the three.

Tradeoff

Dark-only reads as niche and is less inviting for non-technical users. The glow and dot-grid need restraint to avoid feeling gimmicky. More CSS to get right.

Download

Persistent action bar under the QR: PNG / SVG / Copy, each with a glyph, plus a live size · EC readout.
